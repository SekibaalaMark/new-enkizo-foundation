What We Do

Core of Our Approach

“Our approach is personal, dedicated, and sustainable. By partnering with trusted local organizations and committing to annual in-person visits, we aim to ensure our support is effective, transparent, and tailored to the true needs of the youth.”

1. Collaboration with Local Partners

We work closely with the Yesu Foundation, an organization with deep knowledge of the local community and the students’ circumstances. Acting as our eyes and ears on the ground, they handle the selection, guidance, and ongoing follow-up of the students. This close partnership ensures our activities always align with local needs and context.

2. Annual Visits to Uganda

Board members aim to visit the project in Uganda every year. During these visits, we meet directly with students, local partners, and other stakeholders to monitor progress and impact. These face-to-face interactions strengthen relationships while ensuring transparency and mutual trust.

3. Transparent Operations

All resources are used responsibly. We maintain close communication with our partners and receive regular progress reports. Donors and supporters are kept informed of impact stories and milestones from Uganda. Additionally, donors will have the opportunity to visit the project in person.

4. Long-Term Commitment

Our support extends beyond their graduation; we continue to assist students as they transition into employment or start their own businesses. Alumni are encouraged to give back by serving as role models and mentors for incoming students.

Projects

Over the next three years, the foundation aims to build upon its initial achievements and strengthen its foundation. Specifically, this entails:

1. Expanding Student Enrollment

Growing from 3 to a minimum of 5 to 10 supported students, with the goal of guaranteeing sponsorship for the full duration of their studies (a minimum of 3 years).

2. Securing Healthcare

Establishing a dedicated fund for unforeseen medical expenses and student well-being.

3. Fostering Local Independence

  • Supporting our local partner financially so that, starting in 2026, he becomes fully self-sustaining in terms of time and income. We will support him with the coffee plantation so that he can earn a future salary from it.
  • Encouraging students to engage in micro-entrepreneurship initiatives.

4. Strengthening the Foundation in the Netherlands

Strengthening organizational governance, donor relations, and long-term fundraising capabilities in the Netherlands.
