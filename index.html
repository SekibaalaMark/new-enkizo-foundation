Welcome to Enkizo Foundation

We are dedicated to making a positive impact. Our goal is to support projects and empower young people. Join us in our mission to create a better world.

Our Goal

The foundation aims not only to provide students in Uganda with access to quality education, but also to support their holistic development by funding their studies, promoting their mental and physical health, and offering career guidance, in close collaboration with local partners.

Through this, the foundation seeks to contribute to a generation that can build a sustainable future with confidence, self-reliance, and well-being—for themselves, their families, and their communities.

Organization

“The board operates collaboratively and transparently, where all voices are equal and decisions are made thoughtfully in the best interests of the foundation and the youth in Uganda.”

1. Composition

The foundation’s board consists of three members. Each board member brings their own knowledge, experience, and commitment to advance the foundation’s objectives.

2. Equality

All board members have an equal vote. Regardless of role or area of focus, every vote carries equal weight. Decisions are preferably made by consensus. If this is not possible, a majority vote applies.

3. Meetings

The board meets several times a year, both in person and online, to discuss the progress of activities and make decisions. During meetings, the progress of projects in Uganda is reviewed, finances are evaluated, and policy is adjusted where necessary. Minutes are taken for every meeting and formally approved by the board.

4. Duties and Responsibilities

The board is responsible for determining and executing policy, managing resources, and safeguarding the foundation’s objectives. Board members work on a voluntary basis and receive no remuneration for their work, except for the reimbursement of expenses incurred. The board ensures compliance with laws and regulations and provides transparent accountability of activities to donors and partners.

5. Governance Continuity

To ensure continuity, the board may expand in the future or recruit successors. New board members are appointed by the sitting board and are expected to endorse the foundation’s mission and vision.

Plans

“Our plans are focused on creating sustainable impact by providing young people in Uganda not only with financial support for their studies, but also by guiding them in health, well-being, and career development. Through close collaboration with local partners, we strengthen students’ self-reliance and increase the likelihood that they will make a valuable contribution to their communities after completing their studies.”

A strategy for the Enkizo Foundation is built around 4 pillars:

1. Education & Academic Support

  • Providing scholarships to talented students facing financial constraints.
  • Working in close collaboration with local educational institutions to select the right candidates.
  • Offering mentorship and guidance programs to ensure students successfully complete their studies.

2. Health & Well-being

  • Guaranteeing access to basic healthcare for students.
  • Promoting mental health through counseling, workshops, and peer support.
  • Encouraging preventive programs centered on nutrition, hygiene, and sports.

3. Career & Future Prospects

  • Offering vocational orientation and soft/practical skills training.
  • Networking with local businesses and organizations to create and expand internship and employment opportunities.
  • Providing coaching and guidance for finding a first job or launching a business venture.

4. Collaboration & Sustainability

  • Partnering with local organizations to embed long-term impact within the community.
  • Engaging alumni as mentors and role models.
  • Providing transparent reporting of results to donors and partners.
  • Investing in sustainable solutions (such as local knowledge transfer, rather than relying solely on financial aid).
